Playing on a phone or tablet?

How to add as app

Launch FoxVex from a Home Screen icon for more room to play.

  1. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Turn on Open as Web App if offered, then tap Add.
  3. Open FoxVex from its new Home Screen icon.
  1. In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu.
  2. Choose Install and create shortcut or Add to Home screen, then follow the prompts.
  3. Open FoxVex from its new Home Screen icon.

Optionally rotate your screen to landscape and see related layout options in Settings.

New Puzzle

Starting a new puzzle will replace your current progress.

Settings

Allows mismatches and outlines both tiles in red.

Outlines tiles in cyan. Lime notches mark the sides that must face out because they have no match.

On the board, double-tap, double-click, or press M on a focused tile to mark or unmark it. Locked tiles stay put until unlocked.

Auto stacks in portrait and places the tray on the left in landscape. Your preferred layout temporarily adjusts if the other arrangement keeps tiles more readable.

Choose larger tiles with scrolling or let tiles shrink further to fit more of the puzzle on screen.

Tap the FoxVex logo to pause or resume music. Double-tap it to turn music off or restore your last repeat mode.

How to Play

Drag tiles onto the board so the numbers on touching sides match. Drop onto an occupied square to swap tiles.

Cheat Mode outlines edge tiles in cyan. Each lime notch marks a side that must face the outside of the board. The hints stay with the tile when you move it.
